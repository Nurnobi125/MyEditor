# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MyEditor contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and differing perspectives in good faith.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub contact options. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible.
