FFmpeg (downloaded by the app at first run)
MyEditor does not include FFmpeg in its published ZIP. The app offers to download the FFmpeg Essentials Windows build from Gyan Doshi's gyan.dev distribution, with a GitHub mirror fallback, and extracts ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe into the user's local application data folder. The current upstream Essentials build is GPLv3 and includes GPL-licensed codecs such as libx264.
